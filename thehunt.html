The Hunt

It started in Vancouver, Washington. I sourced at the Goodwill bins, dug through thrift racks and checked every store I could get to.

I loved going to vintage market events, and once I'd built up the brand, I started selling at them too. Along the way I got to know other sellers across the area.

Then my junior year I had to move to Coeur d'Alene and start over. So I did the same thing again: found new spots, learned the area, and kept building. I started doing events around Spokane and Coeur d'Alene and built an Instagram to market the brand.

Now I'm at Gonzaga, and school takes most of my time, so I don't hunt like I used to. But thrifting is still my biggest hobby, and it's hard for me to walk past a thrift store without checking the racks.
